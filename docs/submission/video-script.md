## Capture checklist

- Use LIVE server state. Never use a fixture screen as evidence of a real action, mail scan or notification.
- Preserve the transition evidence actually available. Show the earlier approved Calendar result honestly; do not splice a present-day approval tap to that old completion.
- Keep account addresses, private source mail, access codes, credentials, provider resource identifiers and push tokens out of the recording. Cropping or masking must not change the claimed state or result.
- The final edit selects genuine English-only source/plan/completion crops and the actual English routine screen. Historical records stay unchanged; cropping must preserve the evidence and state being claimed.
- A source-referenced 15-minute deadline marker is not the duration of the underlying task. Do not describe it as a scheduled work session.
- Show PREPARE_ONLY and the separate approval boundary. The routine may prepare a fresh Calendar draft but cannot execute it from activation alone.
- If reusing the verified push capture, label its date/evidence context; do not imply a naturally arriving routine mail caused that notification.
- The standalone APK and fresh-account signup/sign-in/Gmail path are verified. Public download and Google allowlisting/contact arrangements are still pending; do not imply anyone can connect Google immediately. Fresh-account Calendar consent and a new push check remain open.
- The latest recorded checks passed 1,919 Python tests and 577 mobile tests across 41 suites. The latest original-account scan completed with 62 messages and an optional-action preparation warning. Preserve that warning; do not present the scan as zero-warning or proof of mailbox-wide accuracy.
